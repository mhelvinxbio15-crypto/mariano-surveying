About Us

Professional Land Surveying Services You Can Trust

Mariano Land Surveying & Realty Services is a licensed Geodetic Engineering and Real Estate Surveying firm based in Manaoag, Pangasinan, Philippines. We specialize in providing accurate, reliable, and legally compliant land surveying solutions.

With years of experience serving residential, commercial, and agricultural clients across Pangasinan, we pride ourselves on delivering professional services with precision and integrity. Our team uses state-of-the-art surveying equipment and follows strict industry standards to ensure every project meets the highest quality requirements.

We are committed to helping property owners, developers, and businesses with accurate land measurements, legal documentation, and expert consultation for all their surveying needs.

Our Services

Comprehensive Land Surveying Solutions

From inquiry to final delivery, we ensure a smooth and professional experience

Relocation Survey

- Accurate boundary relocation and verification of lot corners.

  • • Agricultural zones are shown with green fields and cultivated parcels.

  • • Residential zones are organized into smaller housing blocks with a clear grid.

  • • Commercial zones stand out with larger parcels and taller structures.

  • • Surveying equipment (like a total station) reinforces the geodetic engineer's role in precision mapping.

Subdivision Survey

- Subdivision planning and DENR-compliant technical descriptions.

  • • DENR-compliant technical descriptions like bearings, distances, and lot numbers.

  • • Subdivision layout with glowing parcel outlines and curved road networks.

  • • Geodetic engineer operating a total station, reinforcing your role in precision mapping.

  • • Contour lines and grids emphasize accuracy and technical detail.

Topographic Survey

- Contour mapping, elevation profiles, and site development plans.

  • • Contour mapping with glowing elevation lines and labeled height points.

  • • Elevation profile chart embedded in the terrain, showing slope and relief.

  • • Site development overlays like roads, drainage paths, and building footprints.

  • • Geodetic engineer operating a total station, reinforcing your role in precision terrain analysis.

Verification Survey

- Lot verification and relocation prior to titling and construction.

  • • Highlighted lot boundaries with bearings, distances, and relocation markers.

  • • DENR-compliant annotations for titling and construction readiness.

  • • Geodetic engineer operating a total station, reinforcing your role in boundary validation.

  • • Comprehensive survey reports and digital maps, providing clear documentation for stakeholders and future development.

Land Mapping

- Accurate land mapping for agricultural, residential, and commercial use.

  • • Agricultural zones with green fields and crop patterns.

  • • Residential blocks laid out in a clean grid.

  • • Commercial zones with larger parcels and building footprints.

  • • Surveyor with total station reinforcing your geodetic expertise.
